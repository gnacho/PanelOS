The mark

Five forms, one look

Mark

Where the name is already present or space is small: favicons, the panel's status line, small print.

App icon

The mark on an Ink badge with continuous corners (a superellipse). Home Assistant's icon, avatars, launchers.

Logo

Mark and wordmark side by side: the first choice everywhere else.

Stacked logo

For square or narrow spaces: covers, the panel's boot screen.

Signature

The whole short name, P O S, in Morse on one line. A rule or a closing line, never instead of the logo.

The logo's wordmark is Space Grotesk Medium. Its cap height is 4u, so the mark is three quarters of it, and the mark stands 2u before the P with its centre on the middle of the cap height. Stacked, the mark is 1.6 times larger and centred 3.2u above the cap line. These proportions were chosen in use; they are not changed.

The mark

What not to do

The mark as it is: two Signal dots, two dashes in the ground's text colour, in this order, upright. Everything below changes one of these.
Swapped colours

The dots are always Signal: they are the lit part of the code.

Changed order

Dash and dot first would no longer read as P.

Turned

Never a quarter turn, a mirror image or a tilt. A half turn is the same mark.

Stretched

Never stretched or squashed: dots stay round, dashes 3 units long.

Outlined

Always solid. No outlines, no hollow versions.

Effects

No shadows, gradients or bevels. The glow belongs to the start-up only.

Other colours

Signal and Ink, Paper or white. No other colours, even for a theme.

Other typeface

The wordmark is the outlined Space Grotesk file, never retyped.

In use

On the panel

  • Start-up: the stacked logo on Night with the quiet pattern, written in by the start-up sequence. The same picture is the app's first frame, so nothing jumps.
  • Screensaver and dashboard: no logo. The panel shows the home, not itself.
  • Setup and pairing carry the PanelOS logo, small, at the top; no other maker's logo appears on these screens. The accent is Signal, the grounds Night and Graphite, the text Paper: the palette of these guidelines throughout.
  • Settings: wherever the panel offers its settings, the button is the PanelOS mark, not a gear.
